How the honours work +

These are live season honours, purely for bragging rights. Tied holders share their award. An award appears once there is a qualifying achievement.

Prediction awards use final matches from your league’s starting Gameweek. Copycats and The Entertainer compare the effective main prediction, including a forced score or Power of God replacement; extra Hedge predictions do not inflate these totals. Hit The Post! counts each match once and excludes matches credited with an exact score.

Nostradamus and Best Prediction use credited Correct Scores. Against The World requires a credited Correct Result, a submission from every other member, and no correct outcome among their effective predictions.

Star Man honours include revealed picks only. Power and Curse totals count actual plays once, including vetoed curses, but exclude cancelled plays and unfinished swaps or steals. Super Cards do not count as Power Cards.

Gameweek Champion uses the same UC points as the league statistics, including bonuses. A week counts after every non-postponed fixture has a final result. Every tied weekly leader earns one win. Corrected results and stats automatically update the honours.
